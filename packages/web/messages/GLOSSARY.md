# Adag glossary for the five languages

The chosen word for each term, in every language the app speaks. Every catalog string is translated against this
list, so one concept keeps one word across screens, errors, Telegram alerts and the CSV export.

Never translated, in any language: Adag, USDC, EURC, cirBTC, Morpho, Morpho Blue, Arc, Safe, Circle, Chainlink,
Telegram, Reown, QuickNode, Memo, Multicall3From, Sourcify, MetaMask, Rabby, AdagBills, AdagGuard, and the Tamil
word adagu. "bitcoin" is translated. Amounts, percentages, addresses and hashes are written exactly as in English.

Tone: plain and direct, short sentences. Japanese in desu/masu. Korean in haeyo-che, the way Toss writes. Chinese
simplified, 您, plain. Spanish neutral Latin American with tú. No dash of the U+2014 or U+2015 kind in any language
(the Chinese and Japanese double dash included: use a colon, a comma or a full stop), no emoji, no hype words.

Writing rules per language, beyond the table.

- All: the English "cash" means the stablecoin balance, so it is translated as balance; real cash appears only in the
  India gold story. "Is off" for an unavailable feature is "cannot be used"; "off" is for a switch the user set. A
  note on the 140-byte reference limit says that characters outside plain English take several bytes each, so fewer
  than the limit fit, without adding a digit.
- zh-CN: 网址 for the address of the website, 地址 for wallets and contracts. 借出 means lend and appears only in
  可借出 and "not lent out". A loan against X is 以 X 为抵押品, never a bare 抵押 verb. The guard "acts" is 触发,
  never 行动. 提案 is a noun; the verb is 提交提案. Words retired: 手续费, 构建, 封禁, 门限, 开放, 落地, 安全边际.
- ko: 거래처 for supplier, 인보이스 for invoice (송장 is a parcel waybill). 발동선 is the trigger and any "line you
  choose"; 기준선 is the 40% line only. 작동 only for "how it works" and things that run; the guard "steps in" is
  개입. 일시 중단, never 일시 정지. 읽다 for a person reading a note; 불러오다 for data read from Arc. 보관 only for
  custody. Join lists with a comma, not 및. No hard-coded particles after a placeholder: after {symbol} (USDC, EURC,
  cirBTC end in a vowel sound) write 가, 를, 는, 로, 예요; for bill ids write "{id}번 청구서가"; elsewhere
  restructure. Never put 만 right after a number placeholder.
- ja: Arabic digits with a space before the counter (1 回, 10 件, 5 つ); kanji only where the English has no spelled
  number. 発動 for the guard acting, 動作 only for "behaved as expected". 借入残高 for what is owed, never 負債.
  清算 only for liquidation; use 返済 or 完済 for clear. 現金 only in the India gold story. Avoid the calques "opens"
  (開きます) and "does not read as" (として読めません).
- es: Latin American verbs: agregar, presionar, ingresar, obtener (not añadir, pulsar, introducir, conseguir).
  "monto aprobado" in guard text; "aprobación" only for the on-chain approve action. "mayor que cero", not "más de
  cero". Never "devolver" without an object. "dueño" for a contract owner, "propietario" for a Safe owner. Nav and
  tab labels are short (Docs, Guardián, Cerrar préstamo, Devolver parte, Recuperar bitcoin, Agregar cirBTC); sentences
  use the long forms.

Claims. The English is careful about what Adag promises: the 40% cap, Morpho's 86% line, "never sold", "nothing is
signed or sent", what the guard does and does not guarantee, "best effort" alerts. A translation keeps each claim at
exactly the same strength: it may not soften it, strengthen it or leave it out. The guard keeps a loan away from
Morpho's 86% line; no language says it prevents liquidation.
